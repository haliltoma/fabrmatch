# Privacy notice

> DRAFT — pending legal review (KVKK / GDPR). Describes current practice.

## What we collect
- Account: name, e-mail, password (stored hashed), optional two-factor secret (encrypted).
- Orders: delivery address (encrypted), the models you upload, messages between buyer and maker.
- Makers and sellers: business name, tax number and bank details (encrypted).
- Technical: sign-in sessions (device, address), campaign source of your first visit (labels only).

## Who sees what
- Makers see only the delivery details needed to ship. Buyers never see who the maker is.
- Our payment provider processes payments; we do not store card numbers.

## Your rights
- Download your data and delete your account at any time from Account → Your data.
- Payment and order records are kept without personal details because accounting law requires it.

## Marketing e-mail
Only with your explicit consent, which you can withdraw.
