# Terms of use

> DRAFT — pending legal review. This text describes how Fabrmatch works today; it is not final legal wording.

## What Fabrmatch is
Fabrmatch is a marketplace. Buyers order 3D-printed parts; independent makers produce them; sellers may list products. Fabrmatch matches orders to makers, holds the payment and settles it.

## Accounts
- You must verify your e-mail before you can place an order, pay, open a dispute or receive a payout.
- Keep your password and two-factor codes private. Tell us if you suspect misuse.

## Orders and payment
- Your payment is held by our payment provider while the part is made and delivered.
- After delivery you have 7 days to confirm or to open a dispute. If you do nothing, the order completes and the maker is paid.
- While a dispute is open, no payout is released for that order.

## Models you upload
- Upload only designs you have the right to have printed. Weapons, unsafe items and other people's protected designs are not allowed and may be removed.
- Makers receive your file only for the job, through a time-limited link.

## Makers and sellers
- Makers set their own material prices and are responsible for the quality of what they ship.
- Buyers and makers stay anonymous to each other; keep conversations on the platform.

## Changes
We may update these terms. The version you accepted is recorded.
