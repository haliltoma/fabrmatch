# Cancellation and refunds

> DRAFT — pending legal review.

- Before a maker accepts your job you can cancel and are refunded in full.
- Once production has started the job cannot be cancelled; if there is a problem, open a dispute.
- A dispute can end in a full refund, a partial refund, a reprint by another maker, or release of the payment when no fault is found.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method.
