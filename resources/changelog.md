# Changelog

## 2026-09 — Sizes, carts, and safer accounts
- Order several parts in one cart; they ship in one parcel and one price.
- Pick a print quality (layer height, infill) and, for shop products, a size.
- Delivery estimates and shipping prices that follow your address and the weight of the parcel.
- Message your maker or buyer on the order page; phone numbers and links are hidden automatically.
- Two-factor sign-in, a list of your sessions, and one-click download or deletion of your data.

## 2026-09 — For makers and sellers
- Makers see a scorecard (on-time rate, rating, disputes) and their payout history.
- A photo of the finished part is required before shipping and is kept as evidence if there is a problem.
- Sellers can preview what a margin earns, order a sample of their own design, and see sales analytics.
- Disputes can end in a reprint by another maker with the payment kept on hold.

## 2026-09 — Launch preparation
- Help centre, legal pages (drafts), and pages for makers and sellers to join the waiting list.
